# Service readiness and acceptance criteria

Reviewed against commit `099614f76e93312e3b949a1b5c61c51cbadeb67f` (14 October 2026). This is an investor/partner review prototype. Working software below means a local application operation, never evidence of staffed care or a live provider service. All demonstrations use fictional information. Production startup remains blocked by `apps/api/src/app.ts`; the requirements in [RELEASE.md](RELEASE.md) remain mandatory.

## Commercial proposition

Connect R995/month, Monitor R1,700/month, Concierge R2,650/month; home visits R1,850 per completed visit. Amounts remain VAT inclusive. Medication and external professional services are separate; medical-aid reimbursement depends on scheme approval. The intended benefits and tier inheritance are preserved in `packages/core/domain.ts`, explicitly marked **Planned**. Neither a plan selection nor a paid test invoice buys healthcare. The loan devices, unlimited consultations, nurse reviews, named coordinator, delivery targets and clinic access require operational capacity and agreed service terms before launch.

## Demo acceptance criteria

1. **Subscription experience:** Every catalogue benefit is visibly planned, including inherited benefits. Registration, plan selection and billing explain that selection/test payment cannot activate real care. Approved prices, VAT, home-visit charge and cancellation terms remain unchanged. Local `/api/config`, shared `/config`, web and mobile use the same current catalogue; generated bundles/fixtures must not retain old unqualified promises.
2. **Registration:** Before data entry, web and mobile identify the prototype and prohibit real patient/financial data. Local valid fictional registration requires care consent and an in-area location, verifies a local one-use OTP and creates an empty record with pending membership. Invalid inputs, expired/replayed OTPs and unauthorized access fail. Shared registration/OTP actions fail clearly rather than pretending an account or message was created.
3. **Billing:** Plan changes respect local next-cycle rules; fixed-price invoices and completed-home-visit charges remain test records. Test payment is limited to due invoices, does not accept card/bank details, and reports that no money was charged. Duplicate/repeated payment and billing processing must not duplicate activation or invoices. Cancellation uses the existing business-day notice logic. Shared changes remain temporary.
4. **Clinical workflows:** Reading flags, reviews, escalations, consultation requests and signed notes are reviewable using fictional records and permitted roles. Visible context says no clinician is contacted and no staffed monitoring, diagnosis, treatment or prescription service is provided. Locked notes and restricted records remain protected by local API checks. Delivery tracking denotes a simulation and recipient-name confirmation is never presented as production proof of delivery. Existing emergency guidance remains available.
5. **Integrations:** A fictional consultation, order, claim, reminder or invoice must never be described as a successful external provider transaction. Unsupported shared actions explain their limitation. No live provider credentials or runtime records enter fixtures or builds. No production gate is relaxed by this copy change.

## Production acceptance gates (all remain open)

| Area                                   | Required evidence before service claims become operational                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Subscription                           | Commercial and clinical owners approve benefit scope, fair-use/availability terms, staffed hours, geographic coverage, loan-device terms and delivery targets. | Registration and data                  | Verified OTP delivery, replay/expiry/abuse tests, staff MFA/provisioning, consent and patient identity checks, authorization isolation, retention/privacy handling and independent security approval. | Billing                                | Provider sandbox then approved production evidence: tokenized payment/mandate consent, signature-verified and replay-safe webhooks, idempotency, success/failure/refund handling, reconciliation, recurring billing, invoice delivery, cancellations and failed-payment recovery. Real service activation must follow verified collection and operational readiness, never a test endpoint. |
| Clinical operations                    | Accountable clinical owner approves protocols, device thresholds and escalation response times, after-hours/emergency arrangements, clinician credentials/capacity and documentation. Demonstrate requests reaching real assigned staff, reviews and escalations within approved targets, secure live consultations and valid prescription authorization.                                   |
| Pharmacy and delivery                  | Contracted providers; validate prescription receipt/acceptance/rejection, dispensing exceptions, stock/fulfilment, consent-limited data sharing, dispatch and confirmed delivery, signature/photo/offline sync, duplicate callbacks, retries and audit history. No successful status without provider evidence.                                                                             |
| Medical aid, devices and notifications | Contracted integrations; test eligibility/benefits and claims acknowledgement/rejection/adjudication, reconciliation and outages. Validate device provenance/member matching/units, delayed or duplicate readings, notification consent/delivery/failures and family scope/revocation.                                                                                                      |
| Release and mobile                     | Test PostgreSQL and restore, incident monitoring, accessibility, physical Android/iOS devices, unreliable networks, sessions/permissions, signing and distribution. Record clinical, security, operations and commercial sign-off in release evidence before a separately reviewed production-enablement change.                                                                            |

Assign an accountable owner, provider, test environment, evidence link and sign-off date to each gate. A mock response or passing local test is not provider acceptance. Reviewers may explore the intended journey; the prototype must remain clearly labelled until every relevant gate is satisfied.
